Tournament Morning

Coach Mara unlocks the gym before sunrise. The tournament does not begin for three hours, but the day is already loud in her head: lineups, rotations, snacks, bus times, ankles, nerves, and the thin whistle of a serve that could change everything.

She has prepared for weeks. Still, a tournament is never one story. It is a dozen small choices arriving out of order.
