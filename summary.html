Project Summary

This project is about a volleyball coach named Mara getting ready for a tournament. The story follows the pressure and care behind coaching: checking the gym, planning rotations, watching warmups, remembering last season, choosing a lineup, and preparing the team for match point.

A nonlinear experience is important because tournament preparation does not happen in a straight line. A coach moves between practical details, memories, strategy, and emotion all at once. By letting the reader choose different links, the story feels more like Mara's actual mindset on tournament morning: focused, interrupted, thoughtful, and always adjusting.
